Creating games was the main reason I got into programming. However, for a good game you needed more than code: graphics, music, sound fx and of course an original idea, just to name a few. Practically, that meant that the dream I had as a kid, to learn how to code and then create a game I could enjoy playing, never became a reality.

But now we have AI models like Fable 5.2, that can code, draw pixel art, compose music and also test the gameplay to make sure your game idea is engaging and fun.

Recently I made a game called [*Lumber Jack*](https://lumber-jack.koenvangilst.nl/). It's inspired by my memories of *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans*[^warcraft], *Stardew Valley*[^stardew], the wonderful book *Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow*[^book] by Gabrielle Zevin, and a little bit of Monty Python. In the game you play as Jack, who lost his job as a software engineer to AI. To provide for his young family, he takes a job as a lumberjack in Finland. The pay is good and the work is engaging. It's good to be out in nature and work with his hands. He didn't ask what the land is for. With two kids, you don't ask.

*Lumber Jack* is what I hoped it would be, but it also leaves me a little dissatisfied. It feels like every game idea I've ever had is now just a few prompts away. You just ask and the AI will write the code, design the most beautiful graphics and music, and it'll even write the dialogs. And to me it doesn't feel cheap or soulless anymore either. Games being created now in mere hours would have been huge successes a few years back. But it's all a little too easy now.

That feeling reminds me of something I also experienced as a kid. After spending way too many hours on the strategy game Civilization[^civ], I learned via a friend that if you hold Shift and press 1234567890, you could go into enemy cities, sell their buildings or see the entire map. This made you as the player so much more powerful and it was great for a while. But it made the game too easy and I quickly lost interest in playing it that way. After that, playing Civilization was never really the same for me again.

With AI, it feels like we all got Shift + 1234567890 for making games. Now any game is possible and 99.9999% of them are worthless.
