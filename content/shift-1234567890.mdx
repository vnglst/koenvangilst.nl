As a kid I started programming because I wanted to create games. But a good game needs more than code: graphics, music, sound effects and of course an original idea. So that dream, to create a game I would enjoy playing myself, never became a reality.

Last weekend I gave it another shot, this time with Fable 5.2 in Claude Code. I made a game called [*Lumber Jack*](https://lumber-jack.koenvangilst.nl/). In the game you play as Jack, who lost his job as a software engineer to AI. To provide for his young family, he takes a job as a lumberjack in Finland. The pay is good and the work is engaging. He likes being out in nature and working with his hands. He didn't ask what the land was for. With two kids, you don't ask.

It's inspired by my memories of *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans*[^warcraft], *Stardew Valley*[^stardew], the wonderful novel *Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow*[^book] by Gabrielle Zevin, and a little bit of Monty Python. There's a satisfying loop of gathering, building and automating, with enough story underneath to make you wonder whether Jack is really the good guy here.

*Lumber Jack* is what I hoped it would be, but it also leaves me a little dissatisfied. Fable wrote the code, designed the graphics and music, and even helped with the dialogue. And to me it doesn't feel cheap or soulless. A few years back, a game like this would have taken a complete team many months. It feels like every game idea I've ever had is now just a few prompts away. But it's all a little too easy.

That feeling reminds me of something from my childhood. After spending way too many hours on Civilization[^civ], a friend told me that if you held Shift and pressed 1234567890, you could enter enemy cities, sell their buildings and see the entire map. It was great for a while. But the game became too easy, and I quickly lost interest. Civilization was never really the same for me after that. With AI, it feels like we all got Shift + 1234567890 for making games.

My 12-year-old self would have been thrilled by all of this. Or maybe, just like with Civilization, he would have lost interest because it had become too easy.
