Jack lost his job as a software engineer to AI. To provide for his young family, he takes a job as a lumberjack in Finland. The pay is good and the work is engaging. It's good to be out in nature and work with his hands. He didn't ask what the land is for. With two kids, you don't ask.

People have been sharing beautiful games, videos, and even songs they created with Fable 5.2 lately, so I gave it a shot myself this weekend. Together with my 11-year-old son, we created a game called [Lumber Jack](https://lumber-jack.koenvangilst.nl), and it exceeded all our expectations. The graphics are beautiful, exactly as we wanted them, and the gameplay is engaging and surprising.

The game is inspired by my memories of *Warcraft* (1994), *Stardew Valley*, the wonderful book *Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow* by Gabrielle Zevin, and a little bit of Monty Python.

It was made as a family project, with audio recordings from me and my son. Even so the result feels like a real game. There is a satisfying loop of gathering, building, and automating, with enough story underneath to make you wonder whether Jack is really the good guy here.
