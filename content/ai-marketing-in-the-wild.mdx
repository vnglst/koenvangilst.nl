AI has not changed much about how we spend our holidays, at least not in the physical world. But it has quietly appeared in one place: local advertising. Restaurants, bars and events now use AI-generated images for menus, signs and posters.

During our summer holidays through Austria, Italy and France, I photographed examples I came across. I suspect this period, in which AI imagery is widespread but still recognisable by its mistakes, is only temporary. Even now, some posters were difficult to judge.
